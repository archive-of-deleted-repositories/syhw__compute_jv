## Stated assumptions

1. **Burn flat at $3M/mo from Jul 2026.** Headcount growth bends this up: at a $5M/mo average, add ~$60M to the Q4-2028 line and pull each runway date in ~1 month.
2. **The $50M seed is treated as fully available at Jul 2026.** If largely consumed by the founding period, runway dates pull in ~2 months.
3. **Zero revenue offset.** $100M ARR from Q2 2027 collecting at scale would materially ease the Series-D pressure, but the Aug-2028 gap is not underwritten on it.
4. **JV base schedule** (T3 owned-campus, online Jun 2028). Taking the T3-colo option moves Rubin live to ~Mar 2028 and shifts ~$30–40M of cash earlier — a Series-C-narrative decision as much as a cost one (scenario in the cost model).
5. **Neocloud bridge** covers Jul 2026 → ~Apr 2027, overlapping T1's March-2027 go-live by ~a month of margin.
6. JV figures are the **member cost schedule only** (outbound); no JV revenue, no T6–T7 options (unpriced), per `project_ampere.md`.
