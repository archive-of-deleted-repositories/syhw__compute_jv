# Project Ampère — for Sequoia

**Confidential · for discussion · assumptions as of 14 July 2026**

## The one line
We and two peer AI labs are building **~100MW of NVIDIA compute in France as a member-owned cooperative** — a firm **~$5.0B** program delivering our training compute at **~⅓ of merchant cost**, with our share funded through the financing rounds you'd be anchoring. The specific ask beyond equity is small in cash and decisive in effect: **your credit support behind our take-or-pay is what makes our compute debt financeable.**

## What it is, in five points
1. **Three labs, equal thirds.** Each member owns and finances its own GPU sleeve in every tranche (ring-fenced — a peer's problems never touch ours); a shared PropCo owns the campus, cost-split ⅓.
2. **Demand is us.** Each member signs a two-leg take-or-pay (reserved-capacity fee payable irrespective of usage). No merchant speculation in the base case; lenders underwrite the contracts plus backer credit support.
3. **Fast, then owned.** GB300 racks in existing colo are **live 6 months after FID (March 2027)**; a purpose-built liquid-cooled campus carries Vera Rubin from 2028. Two further tranches are held as **unpriced options** — no commitment to unfrozen NVIDIA specs.
4. **Cost discipline you can audit.** Every input carries a status label (street estimate / assumption / quote-pending); the whole set regenerates from one model; a funded refresh reserve (~$0.49B by year 5) pre-finances the next hardware generation.
5. **Sovereign tailwind, not dependency.** Base case assumes **zero subsidy** — but the EU AI-Gigafactory program (**17% of eligible capex, $600–850M potential**; call open this summer) plus Bpifrance/CDC co-investment are live upside tracks.

## The asks
1. **Credit support behind our take-or-pay** — LC, guarantee, or committed backstop. The single lever converting a venture-backed lab's commitment into underwritable debt; low cash outlay, disproportionate impact on our cost of capital (first close ~9.5%, stepping down as the platform seasons — the mid-teens-to-investment-grade path for GPU debt is established in the public record).
2. **Anchor the rounds** that fund the sleeve equity ($0.62B firm, drawn tranche-by-tranche against the milestone table above).
3. **Optional:** infra-style co-investment at the PropCo (separately financed; euro data-centre securitization take-out once stabilized).

## Downside posture
This is a cost schedule, not a projection: no revenue assumed anywhere; GPUs never bought more than 6 months before service (slips cost time, not stranded silicon); sleeve isolation + several guarantees + buy-sell handle member distress; the largest open number (Rubin quote, ~$180M swing) is bracketed, not hidden.
