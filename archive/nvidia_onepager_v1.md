# Project Ampère (JV3) — NVIDIA One-Pager

**Confidential · for discussion**

## The one line
Three frontier AI labs are committing to **~$7B of NVIDIA rack-scale systems — ~78,200 GPUs (≈49,700 packages) across GB300, Vera Rubin NVL72, and Rubin Ultra NVL576 — in a 140MW French AI factory**. It is a multi-year, roadmap-spanning, *real-demand* offtake we want NVIDIA to anchor as lead strategic investor, allocation partner, and capacity backstop.

## What NVIDIA gets
- **Committed offtake across the full roadmap.** Seven 20MW tranches: **GB300 now → Vera Rubin NVL72 → Rubin Ultra NVL576 (Kyber, 800VDC)**. T1–T5 are the committed path; T6–T7 are structured options that **convert at Rubin-Ultra spec freeze** — procurement that tracks NVIDIA's actual design cadence instead of fighting it. NVIDIA gets a customer that pulls the *next three generations*, not a one-off.
- **A European sovereign-AI flagship.** A France fast-track site (Dunkerque / Le Bosquel), low-carbon nuclear power, EU InvestAI / Bpifrance capital — directly extends NVIDIA's France strategy alongside the Mistral / MGX / Bpifrance campus.
- **An early Rubin Ultra / 800VDC reference customer.** Tranches 6–7 are among the first **NVL576 / Kyber** deployments in Europe — a showcase for the 800VDC, 600kW-rack architecture and MGX ecosystem.
- **Demand that is *real*, not circular.** The buyers are three operating AI labs consuming the compute for their own products, **self-guaranteeing 100% of demand via take-or-pay**. This is the clean answer to the "vendor-financed demand" critique — end-use is contractual and captive.

## The ask to NVIDIA
1. **Allocation & Elite status.** Priority GB300 allocation for tranches 1–2 (**288 racks — 144 per tranche**) and forward Rubin/Rubin-Ultra slots — ideally **first-"Elite"-tier** treatment for a European AI factory.
2. **Capacity backstop.** An obligation to purchase/redeploy unsold capacity — the **$6.3B CoreWeave backstop is the template**. This is the single most powerful credit enhancement: it tightens our debt toward investment grade and de-risks lenders on GPU residual value.
3. **Strategic equity.** Lead strategic investment at TopCo (the "S2 / NVIDIA-strategic" capitalization) — aligning NVIDIA to the platform's growth, as with CoreWeave, Nebius, and xAI.
4. **Technical partnership.** MGX reference-design, Mission Control, and DGX Cloud / software alignment; co-marketing as a European Rubin flagship.
5. **Vendor financing.** OEM/ODM deferred-payment or lease support (Dell / Supermicro / HPE) to smooth the chip-heavy Rubin tranches.

## Why this de-risks NVIDIA's ecosystem
- **Three creditworthy-anchored labs** (venture-backed, with lead-investor credit support behind their take-or-pay) diversify counterparty risk vs a single-tenant neocloud (CoreWeave was 62–67% one customer).
- **A backstop here is low-risk for NVIDIA:** demand is contracted and captive, France power is cheap and clean, and the sovereign/EU capital layer absorbs ~15–20% of capex — so the assets NVIDIA would ever backstop are already the most-utilized in Europe.
- **Roadmap pull-through:** committing three labs to Rubin Ultra now accelerates NVL576 / 800VDC volume and validates the architecture for the next wave of European buyers.

## Alignment with NVIDIA's Europe strategy
France is NVIDIA's beachhead for sovereign European AI. Project Ampère adds a **second flagship** to the Mistral/MGX effort — three independent labs, a diversified offtake, and an early Rubin-Ultra showcase — reinforcing the "AI factory" narrative on low-carbon, sovereign infrastructure.
