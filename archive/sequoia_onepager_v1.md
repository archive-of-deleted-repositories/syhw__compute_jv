# Project Ampère (JV3) — Sequoia One-Pager

**Confidential · for discussion**

## The one line
Three frontier AI labs are building **140MW of NVIDIA compute in France** as a captive cooperative — each owning and self-guaranteeing its third. We are one of the three, and we want Sequoia behind our sleeve. The ask is small in cash and large in leverage: **your credit support turns our take-or-pay into investment-grade debt**, and secures our portfolio's scarcest input — compute — at cost.

## Why this matters for Sequoia
- **Compute is the moat.** Our sleeve is **~48 GB300 racks now, scaling through Vera Rubin** — guaranteed capacity we control at roughly **half merchant cost** (≈$2.5–3.0 per normalized GB300-GPU-hour-equivalent vs ~$5–8 merchant; later generations priced in normalized compute credits, where our per-credit cost stays flat-to-falling). That discount compounds directly into model iteration speed and gross margin across the AI portfolio.
- **Security of supply.** We're not renting scraps on the spot market or hostage to a neocloud's allocation — we own the racks and the offtake. In a compute-constrained market, that is the difference between shipping and stalling.
- **Infra-grade returns, optionally.** Beyond the strategic case, the JV throws off contracted, hard-asset cash flows (take-or-pay + French low-carbon power + an ABS-able shell). Sequoia can participate at the infra layer for infra returns, or simply enable ours.

## The structure (why it's financeable)
- **Three equal members**, each funds and controls its own **~7MW ChipCo sleeve** per tranche on a **shared campus** (PropCo). One member's problems never touch the others' — clean credit isolation.
- **Demand is 100% self-guaranteed** by the three labs' take-or-pay. No external anchor needed; utilization is locked from day one.
- **The gating item is credit, not cash.** The members are venture-backed (sub-investment-grade), so the debt behind each sleeve needs a backer to stand behind the take-or-pay. **That's the specific ask below.**

## The ask to Sequoia
1. **Credit support behind our take-or-pay** — an LC, guarantee, or committed equity backstop on our sleeve. Debt is sized on the **reserved-capacity leg** of our contract (payable irrespective of usage); your support is the single biggest lever tightening our first-close debt (~SOFR+450–650) and opening the **refinancing path to investment grade** (the CoreWeave DDTL 4.0 benchmark, ~SOFR+2.25% — reached only after seasoning). Low cash outlay, disproportionate financing impact.
2. **Anchor our sleeve equity** — lead/participate in the **~45–50% equity** of our ChipCo (the base capitalization: equity-led, conservatively levered, control retained; EU/sovereign capital and NVIDIA support are upside levers that walk our equity need down, not base-case assumptions).
3. **Optional: infra co-invest at JV level** for contracted infra returns.

## Returns & downside protection
- **Capital-efficient:** each block's **shell (PropCo)** is refinanced after 18–24 months of contracted operating history (euro DC ABS — Vantage's €640M EMEA deal is the template), recycling equity into the next tranche. GPU-side refinancing is treated as upside only. Peak cash outstanding is a fraction of the headline.
- **Downside is hard-backed and staged:** two-leg take-or-pay contracts, a long-life ABS-able shell, French power with a nuclear-PPA negotiation path (€70–105/MWh scenarios), the final two tranches held as **options** (no fixed commitment to unfrozen NVIDIA specs), and up to **~17% of capex in EU/sovereign capital pursued as upside** (InvestAI + Bpifrance — underwritten only on award). NVIDIA is our lead strategic investor and is being asked for a capacity backstop.
- **Strategic value line:** the merchant-vs-cooperative compute discount alone is worth hundreds of $M/yr across the portfolio at scale — often larger than the equity IRR.
