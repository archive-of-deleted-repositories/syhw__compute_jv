The structure

One program, financed as seven ~€1B tranches

We are not financing a single €8B project. Each 20MW tranche is its own bankruptcy-remote SPV — non-recourse to the others and to the four sponsor members — split into a slow asset and a fast asset, sized for debt by the credit of its offtake, and refinanced once stabilized to recycle equity into the next.

PropCo — the powered shell
~€220M, 10–15yr life. Long, cheap infrastructure debt at ~65% LTV, EURIBOR + 250–400bps.
ChipCo — the GPUs
~€780M, 3–5yr life. Morgan Stanley-led senior GPU-backed debt + NVIDIA/OEM vendor finance. Borrowing base = depreciable GPU cost.
OpCo — the operator
Signs take-or-pay with Meta/Nebius and arm's-length contracts with members. One revenue face; pays the SPVs.

The engine

How €100–300M funds a €7–8B build

The consortium equity is revolving downpayment capital, not a cumulative bill. Each tranche is refinanced once it has 12–18 months of contracted history — returning a meaningful slice of equity to redeploy — while an infrastructure co-equity partner funds the bulk of the Vera Rubin phase. The credit basis

Debt is sized by the credit of the offtake — not by nameplate

Demand is captive (the four members) + anchor take-or-pay (Meta, Nebius) + merchant backstop. We route the contracts deliberately: Meta's investment-grade take-or-pay anchors the cheapest, largest senior debt; member demand fills utilization once credit-supported by their backers; merchant is sized at zero for debt and accrues to equity.

Getting started

First 180 days

  1. Form & mandate. Stand up TopCo + T1 SPVs; sign the JV agreement; mandate Morgan Stanley with a warehouse line in scope.
  2. Lock the anchor. Convert Meta / Nebius interest into take-or-pay LOIs — the gating document for senior debt.
  3. Site & allocation. Sign exclusivity at Dunkerque or Le Bosquel; open RTE phasing; confirm GB300 allocation and deposit terms.
  4. Structure the money. Bankable model + IM; senior facility + warehouse; close €100–300M consortium equity; begin co-equity diligence.
Two items gate everything: a Meta/Nebius take-or-pay LOI before debt close, and the Morgan Stanley mandate before any capex is committed. Everything else sequences off those two.
