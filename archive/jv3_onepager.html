What changes vs the base plan

Two moves define JV3

Drop from four members to three, and let the members themselves guarantee the demand instead of anchoring on a Meta/Nebius take-or-pay. It's a clean trade: no external-anchor dependency and 100% of the economics retained — paid for with a weaker (sub-investment-grade) debt credit that has to be shored up.

Base plan (JV1/JV2)

  • 4 members
  • Demand: captive + Meta/Nebius IG anchor + merchant
  • Debt anchored on Meta's investment-grade credit
  • ~27% equity; senior advance ~55–60%
  • Must land an anchor LOI to close debt

JV3

  • 3 equal members, each controls & funds its sleeve
  • Demand: 100% self-guaranteed by the members
  • Debt on sub-IG offtake + credit enhancement
  • ~40% equity; credit-enhanced advance ~40–50%
  • Gate shifts to VC-backer credit support

Capacity architecture

Constant 7MW per member — fewer, denser racks each generation

Each member holds the same ~7MW slice in every tranche. As rack density climbs from GB300 to Rubin to Rubin Ultra, the rack count per member collapses while GPUs-per-rack rise sharply — the same power, radically more compute.

Structure

Three sleeves on one shared campus

Each member's 48 racks and its own take-or-pay sit in a dedicated ChipCo sleeve, so a member's guarantee backs only its own debt. The powered shell, grid and cooling are a shared PropCo, cost-split in equal thirds. A stumble in one sleeve is isolated — it can be re-marketed or bought out without touching the others.

The financing crux

Self-guaranteed demand is certain — but sub-investment-grade

The members are the demand, so utilization is locked in — but they're VC-backed labs, where CoreWeave's debt leaned on Microsoft's investment-grade backlog. JV3 has to manufacture credit around the members' guarantees, and carry more equity.

Credit enhancement — how sub-IG offtake is made bankable

Several member guarantees 6–12mo capacity prepayment → DSRA VC-backer LCs / guarantees / equity NVIDIA capacity backstop Over-collateralization

Concentration is ~33% per member — better than CoreWeave's 62–67% single-tenant, but the three are correlated (all AI labs, same cycle), so lenders apply a correlation haircut. Sleeve isolation + the NVIDIA backstop are what hold the structure. An optional Meta/Nebius anchor or merchant book can be grafted onto a tranche to buy back leverage.
