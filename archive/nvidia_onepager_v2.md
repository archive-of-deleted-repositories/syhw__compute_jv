# Project Ampère — NVIDIA One-Pager

**Confidential · for discussion · assumptions as of 14 July 2026**

## The one line
Three frontier AI labs are committing to a firm **~$3.5B of NVIDIA rack-scale systems and networking** — **558 racks / 40,176 GPU packages / 444 EF of dense FP8** across GB300 and Vera Rubin NVL72 — in a ~100MW French compute cooperative, with two further tranches structured as options that convert at your spec freeze. It is multi-year, roadmap-aligned, *real-demand* offtake we want NVIDIA to anchor as allocation partner, backstop provider, and strategic investor.

## What NVIDIA gets
- **Committed pull across the roadmap, sized honestly.** Firm program: **288 GB300 NVL72 racks now** (T1 buyout at month 0) and **270 Vera Rubin NVL72 racks** on the committed path (re-priced at spec freeze, as it should be). T6–T7 are structured options that convert when Rubin-Ultra's configuration and pricing are frozen — procurement that tracks your design cadence instead of fighting it, and expands the program well beyond $3.5B when struck.
- **Demand that answers the circularity critique.** The buyers are three operating AI labs consuming the compute for their own training runs, committed through **take-or-pay contracts with venture-backer credit support**. No merchant speculation sits in the base case — the cost schedule assumes zero resale revenue.
- **A European sovereign-AI flagship** on French low-carbon power — GB300 live in colo within 6 months (immediate visible deployment), a purpose-built liquid-cooled campus behind it — complementing your existing French AI-campus footprint.
- **A disciplined reference customer.** The program's public-record financial hygiene (dense-FP8 accounting, package counting, status-labelled costs, options instead of vaporware commitments) makes it a credible showcase, not a headline risk.

## The asks
1. **Allocation.** 288 GB300 NVL72 racks (144 per tranche, first buyout at m0, second at m6) and forward Rubin NVL72 slots aligned to campus delivery (m15 and m21/m27 buyouts) — with the integrated-system configuration that supports our 6-month order-to-live schedule.
2. **Capacity backstop.** An NVIDIA obligation to purchase or redeploy unsold capacity — the structure you've used publicly at multi-billion scale. It is the single most powerful credit enhancement for our sleeve debt and costs NVIDIA little here: the capacity is member-consumed by construction.
3. **Strategic equity at TopCo.** Aligning NVIDIA with the platform across generations, as you have with other operator-partners.
4. **Vendor financing.** OEM/ODM deferred or lease terms inside our 63% debt tranche (detailed stack carries a dedicated **23% vendor-finance slice**).
5. **Technical partnership.** Reference architectures for the 227kW MaxP campus blocks and the Rubin-ready colo question (MaxQ 187kW widens the qualifying-facility pool — your guidance directly shapes our T3 siting decision and its timing).

## Why the structure de-risks NVIDIA
Three creditworthy-backed labs at ⅓ each — no single-tenant concentration; ring-fenced per-member sleeves; GPUs bought no more than 6 months before service (construction slips don't strand silicon); a funded refresh reserve (~$1.5B across the JV by year 5) that pre-finances the *next* NVIDIA generation; and an options structure that converts to firm orders exactly when your specs freeze.
