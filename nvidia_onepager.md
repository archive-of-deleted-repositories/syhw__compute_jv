# Project Ampère — for NVIDIA

**Confidential · for discussion · assumptions as of 14 July 2026**

## The one line
Three frontier AI labs are committing to a firm **~$3.5B of NVIDIA rack-scale systems and networking** — **558 racks, 40,176 GPU packages, 444 EF of dense FP8** across GB300 and Vera Rubin NVL72 — in a ~100MW French compute cooperative, with two further tranches structured as **options that convert at your spec freeze**, and a live path into the **EU AI-Gigafactory program**. We want NVIDIA as allocation partner, backstop provider, and strategic investor.

## Why this deal is clean for NVIDIA
1. **Real demand, contractually.** The buyers are three operating labs consuming the compute for their own training, committed through take-or-pay with venture-backer credit support. Zero merchant resale in the base case — the clean answer to the circularity critique.
2. **Orders that track your roadmap.** Firm: **288 GB300 NVL72 racks** (first buyout at program month 0, second at month 6) and **270 Vera Rubin NVL72 racks** re-priced at spec freeze. T6–T7 convert to firm orders only when Rubin-Ultra's configuration and pricing are frozen — no vaporware commitments, no headline risk.
3. **A European flagship with public backing in motion.** French low-carbon power, GB300 visible in colo within 6 months, and a **EuroHPC Gigafactory bid track** (17% capex contribution; call open this summer; Bpifrance/CDC co-investment in parallel) — complementing your existing French AI-campus footprint rather than competing with it.
4. **Financial hygiene that survives diligence.** Dense-FP8 accounting, package counting, status-labelled costs, options instead of promises, a funded refresh reserve (~$1.5B across the JV by year 5) that pre-finances the *next* NVIDIA generation.

## The asks
1. **Allocation.** 288 GB300 NVL72 racks (144 + 144, buyouts at m0 and m6 — i.e., allocation confirmed **before September 2026**), and forward Rubin slots aligned to campus delivery (buyouts m15, m21, m27), in integrated-system configuration supporting a 6-month order-to-live schedule.
2. **Capacity backstop.** An NVIDIA purchase/redeploy obligation on unsold capacity — the structure you've used publicly at multi-billion scale. It is the single most powerful credit enhancement for the members' sleeve debt, and low-risk here: capacity is member-consumed by construction.
3. **Strategic equity at TopCo**, aligning NVIDIA across generations as with your other operator-partners.
4. **Vendor financing** — OEM/ODM deferred or lease terms; the detailed capital stack carries a dedicated **23% vendor-finance slice**.
5. **Technical partnership** — reference architecture for the 227kW MaxP campus blocks, and a **MaxQ (187kW) colo-feasibility workshop**: whether Rubin can run MaxQ in existing European liquid-cooled colo directly decides if the first Rubin tranche lands in **March vs June 2028**. Your engineers shape our schedule.

## Why the structure de-risks NVIDIA
Three credit-supported buyers at ⅓ each (no single-tenant concentration) · ring-fenced per-member sleeves · GPUs bought no more than 6 months before service (construction slips never strand silicon) · refresh reserve pre-funding the next generation · options converting exactly when your specs freeze · and a public-capital track (EU 17% + Bpifrance) that strengthens the buyers' balance sheets without touching NVIDIA's terms.
